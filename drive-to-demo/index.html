01 · Brand Positioning Strategy

From "AI tool" to workshop productivity partner

Service leaders do not buy AI. They buy fewer comebacks, faster repairs and technicians who get productive sooner. The positioning led with those business outcomes.

For dealership and fleet service heads who lose hours to repeat repairs and skills gaps, the platform guides every technician through each repair step so jobs are done right the first time.

Branded search impressions up 40% by the end of the quarter.

05 · Profitability model

The maths behind every budget call

Assumes $15,000 first-year contract value per pilot and a 70% gross margin, typical for B2B software.

ROAS

ROAS = Contract value ÷ Ad spend = $30,000 ÷ $12,000
2.5x (250%)

DecisionCampaign is returning well above spend in year one.

Net ROI

Gross profit = $30,000 × 70% = $21,000 Net ROI = ($21,000 − $12,000) ÷ $12,000
75% net ROI

DecisionProfitable after delivery costs, before counting renewals.

Break-even ROAS

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ Gross margin = 1 ÷ 0.70
1.43x

DecisionAny channel below 1.43x loses money and gets cut or reworked.

Max CPC bid

Max CPC = (Contract value ÷ Target ROAS) × Click-to-deal rate = ($15,000 ÷ 2.0) × (2 ÷ 2,350)
$6.38 per click ceiling

DecisionLinkedIn at $6.36 sits right at the ceiling. Tighten targeting before adding budget.

Revenue per click (RPC) by channel

RPC = Contract value ÷ Clicks = $30,000 ÷ 2,350 = $12.77 Target max CPC = RPC ÷ Target ROAS 2.5x = $5.11 Break-even CPC = RPC ÷ 1.43x = $8.94 Max cost per deal = $15,000 ÷ 2.5 = $6,000
$5.11 target CPC · $8.94 break-even

DecisionGoogle Search ($4.00) beats the target, so it gets more budget next quarter. LinkedIn ($6.36) is profitable but above target, so it stays for awareness with narrower targeting.

06 · Growth plan

Next 90 days

  1. Move 40% more budget into Google Search, where cost per click is below target.
  2. Narrow LinkedIn to service heads at dealer groups with 5+ workshops to bring CPC under $5.11.
  3. Turn the 2 pilots into case studies and use them as proof in the nurture sequence.
  4. Run account-based campaigns for 25 named OEM after-sales and fleet accounts.
  5. Review ROAS, CPL and pipeline weekly, and scale only what runs above break-even.
